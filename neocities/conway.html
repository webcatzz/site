rules

tl;dr select a color and click to place clusters. kill everything that isn't you

the game follows standard conway rules, with some others tossed in:

  1. any cell with less than two neighbors dies.
  2. any cell with more than three neighbors dies.
  3. any empty space with exactly three neighbors spawns a cell.
  4. you may spawn a cluster of cells once every five seconds.
  5. newly spawned cells inherit their neighbors' most common color.
  6. enemy clusters spawn randomly.
