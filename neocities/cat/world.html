the world of aerie

aerie is the planet that serves as the comic's setting! it's split into two parts: belowcloud, flooded and irradiated, and abovecloud, the stage upon which our story takes place.

although similar to earth, global warming and the shattering left aerie with fifteen-hour days, darker nights, a more extreme climate, and massive flooding.

cities of note

  • mneme, jay's home city
  • dallas, where our story begins
  • eden, the shining city
