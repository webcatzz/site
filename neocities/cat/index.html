welcome to cat comic >

cat comic is a worldbuilding and comic project i began on april 13th, 2023. it will, hopefully, be a heavily annotated comic (and then some) about identity, history and the end of the world.

inspirations of varying degree: daisy in dreamland, homestuck, laika's comet, fear not!, 17776, eastward, and any other work ever made.

this page is intended as a somewhat-comprehensive store of knowledge for all things cat comic. you can quickly navigate to a few important pages using the sidebar on the left, although there are plenty of unlisted pages. don't treat everything here as gospel—cat comic is a continually evolving work (read: it's still a work in progress).
