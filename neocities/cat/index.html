under construction!

welcome to cat comic, a worldbuilding/comic project of mine i began on april 13th, 2023. cat comic takes place in the world of aerie, in which the surface was made uninhabitable long ago—however, civilization endures among the clouds!

cat comic is a collection of thematically related and heavily annotated stories about the apocalypse. cat comic is going to be a long and slow project—very long, if it even manages to get anywhere near ready to publish. i give no guarantee it will reach that stage. instead: enjoy the process!

inspirations: homestuck, daisy in dreamland, laika's comet
