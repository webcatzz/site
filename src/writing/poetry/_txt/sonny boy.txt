jun 28, 2024

in the house of a gambler
the old man claws my face
drizzling spittle from cracked lips
he dissolves
gapes and gasps and revvs into a croak
“boy, sonny boy,”
—fingers straining tight my loose skin—
“kiss me: i love you,
i love you,
i love you,”
abruptly releases me
and casts his hand to heaven
eyes glossy as his hard-won marbles
reflecting the soft orange of a cigarette butt below
the fire does not spare him when it comes
