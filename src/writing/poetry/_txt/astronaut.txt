no clue

the armored stranger
stood among the stalks of wheat
alone, looking up
at the distant stars above.

i asked him, once, "who are you?"
but the stranger gave no answer.
i asked him, once, "what have you seen?"
but the stranger gave no answer.
i asked him, once or twice, many things,
but the stranger gives no answers.

for the stranger has no mouth,
and he cannot speak.
the stranger has no eyes,
and he cannot see.
the stranger has no face:
just a pitch-black mirror
with the universe inside of it.

and when the stranger leans his tired head down
and looks at me with that strange face of his,
and though its gaze has left the starry sky
the mirror is still full of blinding starlight,
i begin to think that maybe the stars
are not quite so distant after all.
