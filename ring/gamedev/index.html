about

hi! welcome! this is a webring for gamedevs on the indie web. hobbyists, professionals, beginners — all are welcome.

seasonally, we host game jams! you don't have to participate to be in the ring and vice versa. more about our jams below.

how to join

thinking of joining? remember, this ring is for websites that...

  • host gamedev-related stuff
  • keep the widget accessible

if you'd like to join...

  • pick a short unique identifier (mine is juneish, for example)
  • add the widget html to your site
    • replace both REPLACEME's in the widget html with your identifier
  • email me (june.webcatz@gmail.com)
    • use the subject line "gamedevring"
    • include your site's url
    • include the identifier you chose

i'll mail you back once you're in :-)
