beepbox webring

what is this
a webring for anyone on the indie web who posts music made with beepbox (or a fork). also a way of finding cool music
rules
you should
  • have a personal site
  • display one of the widgets on your site
  • display your beepbox tracks on your site
how to join
email june.webcatz@gmail.com with (1) the subject line "beepbox webring", (2) your site url, and (3) the url of the page on which you display your beepbox tracks
